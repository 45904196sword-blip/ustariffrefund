Scam Alert List: Protect Your Refund

As billions in refunds are announced, scammers are targeting unsuspecting businesses and individuals. Stay informed.

Current Threat Landscape

The IRS and CBP have issued a joint warning regarding "Tariff Phishing" schemes. These scams often mimic official government correspondence to steal Social Security numbers, EINs, or banking information.

Top 3 Refund Scams

Scam Name Modus Operandi Red Flag
The Fast-Track Fee Asks for a $200-$500 "processing fee" to jump the line. Demands payment via Gift Card or Crypto.
The Verification Link SMS/Email with a link to "verify your identity." Link goes to a non-gov (.com/.net) site.
The IEEPA Phone Call Robocall claiming your refund is "frozen" due to an error. Asks for your bank PIN or password.

How to Verify Official Requests

Official correspondence regarding the 2026 refunds will always come via USPS mail on official IRS or CBP letterhead, or through your secure ACE portal account. If you receive an unsolicited call, hang up and contact the official CBP help desk directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a consultant help me file?

Yes, legitimate customs brokers and attorneys can assist, but they will never ask for payment through unofficial channels like Zelle or Venmo.

Where do I report a scam?

Report all suspicious activity to the Treasury Inspector General for Tax Administration (TIGTA) or via the official CBP portal.
