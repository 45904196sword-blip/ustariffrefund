Top 5 Reasons Your Refund Might Be Denied

Don't let a simple mistake cost you your refund. Learn the most common denial reasons and how to avoid them.

Why Claims Get Rejected

The CBP and IRS maintain strict standards for the 2026 Tariff Refund program. While billions are available, nearly 15% of initial filings are rejected due to administrative errors or eligibility mismatches.

Common Denial Factors

Denial Reason Description Solution
Incorrect HTS Code The product code submitted is not on the eligible List 3 or 4A. Cross-check code with official 2026 Exclusion List.
Incomplete 7501 Form Missing signatures or illegible entry dates. Resubmit high-resolution digital scans.
Statute of Limitations Entries made before 2022 or after the cutoff. Verify "Date of Entry" on your paperwork.
Lack of Proof of Payment Unable to show that the duty was actually paid to CBP. Provide bank transfer or ACE receipt.

What to Do If Denied

If you receive a DEN-04 notice, you have 60 days to file a formal protest (CBP Form 19). Most denials are due to "Missing Information," which can be easily fixed by providing the requested supplemental documents through the ACE portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I re-apply after a denial?

Yes, you can re-apply if the denial was for administrative reasons. If it was for "Legal Ineligibility," you must use the Appeals process.

Does a denial affect my tax standing?

No, a tariff refund denial is an administrative customs matter and does not imply any wrongdoing or audit of your regular income taxes.
