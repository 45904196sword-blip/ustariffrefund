Refund Impact: The Electronics Sector

Electronics were among the hardest hit by Section 301 tariffs. Here is how the 2026 refund affects tech importers and consumers.

Electronics: A High-Value Recovery

From smartphones to industrial circuit boards, the electronics industry paid billions in duties. The 2026 refund rollout provides a significant liquidity boost for tech companies and potential price corrections for consumers.

Consumer Rebates on Tech

Under the American Worker Rebate Act, individuals who purchased high-value electronics between 2023 and 2025 may be eligible for a "Price Correction Credit." This is designed to offset the "tariff tax" passed down by retailers during the height of the trade tensions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my original receipt?

For individual claims, a digital receipt or credit card statement showing the purchase of an eligible electronic item is required.

Are refurbished electronics eligible?

Generally, no. The refund applies to the original import duty paid on new goods entering the US.
