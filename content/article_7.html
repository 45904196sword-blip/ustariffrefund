Refund Impact: Apparel & Fashion

The apparel industry faced unique challenges with Section 301. Discover how the 2026 refund relief is being distributed.

Apparel: Navigating Complex Classifications

Apparel imports are subject to some of the most complex HTS classifications. The 2026 refund program specifically targets List 4A items, which included many clothing categories that were previously exempted or subject to lower rates before 2022.

Eligible Apparel Items

Item Type Refund Type Documentation
Cotton Finished Goods Full Duty Drawback Entry Summary (7501)
Synthetic Sportswear Partial Exclusion Certificate of Origin
Footwear (Non-Leather) Full Duty Drawback Bill of Lading

Special Provisions for Retailers

Small and medium-sized fashion boutiques that imported directly from overseas manufacturers can use the CAPE guide to simplify their claims. For larger retailers, the focus is on reconciling massive volumes of entry data to capture every dollar of refundable duty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are luxury goods included?

Yes, provided they were subject to Section 301 duties and the importer can provide the necessary CBP documentation.

What about raw fabrics?

Raw materials are eligible if they were imported for domestic manufacturing and the duty was paid under the specified tariff lists.
