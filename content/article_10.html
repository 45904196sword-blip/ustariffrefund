Step-by-Step Status Tracking Guide

Wondering where your refund is? Follow this guide to track your claim through the official CBP and IRS systems.

Understanding Status Codes

When you log into the ACE portal (for businesses) or the IRS tracker (for individuals), you will see various status codes. Knowing what these mean can save you hours of unnecessary phone calls.

Common Claim Statuses

Status Code Meaning Estimated Time Remaining
REC-01 Claim Received & Initial Validation 4-6 Weeks
AUD-02 Documentation Review (Audit) 2-4 Weeks
APP-03 Approved for Disbursement 7-10 Days
DEN-04 Claim Denied / Needs Correction Action Required

How to Check Your Status

  1. Log In: Access the ACE Portal or IRS.gov/Refund2026.
  2. Enter ID: For businesses, enter your EIN and Entry Number. For individuals, use your SSN and 2025 Tax ID.
  3. View Dashboard: Navigate to the "Section 301 Refund" tab.
  4. Check History: Review the "Audit Log" for any notes from agents requesting more data.

Frequently Asked Questions

My status hasn't changed in 3 weeks. What should I do?

The AUD-02 phase is currently experiencing backlogs. If your status remains static for more than 45 days, you should submit a "Status Inquiry" via the secure messaging center.

Can I track a paper check?

Yes, once the status hits APP-03, a USPS tracking number will be provided if the refund exceeds $1,000.
