Small Business CAPE Guide: Maximizing Your Refund

The Custom Administrative Processing Exclusion (CAPE) is the key for small businesses to reclaim duties paid under Section 301.

What is CAPE for Small Businesses?

The CAPE initiative is designed to simplify the refund process for businesses with annual revenue under $50 million. Instead of complex litigation, CAPE allows for a streamlined verification of "unavoidable duty expenses" incurred between 2022 and 2025.

Verification Requirements

Document Type Purpose
CBP Form 7501 Proof of entry and duty payment.
Commercial Invoices Verification of product classification.
Tax ID (EIN) Confirming business legal status.

How to Apply

Small businesses should first verify their HTS codes against the master list of eligible products. Once verified, applications must be submitted through the Automated Commercial Environment (ACE) portal under the 2026 Refund Provision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply if my business closed in 2025?

Yes, successor entities or former owners may still be eligible for refunds on duties paid while the business was active.

Are service-based businesses eligible?

Typically no, unless they can prove direct import costs for equipment subject to Section 301 tariffs.
