Refund Impact: Furniture & Home Decor

The furniture industry saw significant price hikes due to 25% tariffs. The 2026 refund aims to provide relief to this sector.

Furniture: A Major Refund Opportunity

Furniture was one of the first categories to feel the full weight of the Section 301 tariffs. With a standard 25% duty rate on many Chinese-origin pieces, the potential for high-value refunds is substantial for both retailers and large-scale importers.

High-Impact Furniture Categories

Category Avg. Duty Paid Refund Priority
Wooden Bedroom Furniture 25% + ADD/CVD Very High
Upholstered Seating 25% High

Reclaiming "Double Duties"

Some furniture items were hit with both Section 301 tariffs and Anti-Dumping/Countervailing Duties (ADD/CVD). The 2026 refund process primarily addresses the Section 301 portion, but businesses must ensure their claims correctly isolate these separate duty types to avoid rejection by CBP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are outdoor patio sets eligible?

Yes, most outdoor furniture categories under HTS 9401 and 9403 are included in the refund eligibility list.

What if my furniture was imported through a third country?

If the "Country of Origin" remains China despite transshipment, and Section 301 duties were paid, the item is still eligible for a refund.
