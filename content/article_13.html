2026 Outlook: US-China Trade & Refunds

As the trade landscape shifts, the 2026 refund program serves as a critical pivot point for US-China relations and domestic supply chains.

A New Chapter in Trade Policy

The $166B refund isn't just an administrative correction; it represents a recalibration of the 2018-2025 trade strategy. In 2026, we see a move toward "Precision Tariffs" where blanket duties are replaced by targeted enforcement, and previous over-collections are returned to the economy.

Supply Chain Decoupling or De-risking?

The availability of these refunds is encouraging businesses to re-invest in domestic capacity. By reclaiming capital once tied up in tariffs, companies are better positioned to diversify their sourcing away from high-risk corridors while maintaining essential trade ties with China for critical components.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will tariffs be eliminated in 2026?

Elimination is unlikely, but many List 3 and List 4A items are expected to see permanent exclusions or significant rate reductions as the refund program proceeds.

How does this affect inflation?

Economists predict that the $166B injection could lower consumer price indices (CPI) for durable goods by 0.5% to 1.2% over the next 18 months.
