Digital Filing Tips

All documents should be scanned in PDF format at a minimum of 300 DPI. For businesses with thousands of entries, use the "Batch Upload" feature in the ACE portal and ensure filenames match the Entry Numbers for faster processing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use bank statements instead of receipts?

For individuals, a bank statement showing the retailer name and amount is usually sufficient if the original receipt is lost.

How long should I keep these records?

Federal law requires you to keep all import and tax-related documentation for 5 years after the refund is issued.
