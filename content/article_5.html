Stay ahead of the deadlines. Here is the step-by-step schedule for the $166B tariff refund rollout.

Key Phases of the Refund Process

The refund process is structured into four distinct phases: Announcement, Verification, Disbursement, and Appeals. Each phase has specific requirements for importers and consumers.

2026 Schedule at a Glance

Date Range Phase Action Required
Jan 15 – Mar 30 Registration Update contact info in ACE/IRS portals.
Apr 1 – May 10 Verification CBP reviews import records and duty payments.
May 11 – Jul 30 Disbursement Direct deposits and checks issued to claimants.
Aug 1 – Oct 31 Appeals Submission of protests for denied or partial refunds.

What Happens If You Miss a Deadline?

Missing the registration window (Phase 1) may result in significant delays. However, the American Worker Rebate Act provides a "Late Filer" window in late 2026, though these claims may be subject to additional scrutiny and lower interest rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these dates firm?

While these are the official targets, administrative delays at CBP could shift the disbursement window by 2-4 weeks.

When is the absolute last day to file?

Currently, the final cutoff for all 2026 refund claims is December 15, 2026.
