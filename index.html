Who is getting paid on May 11?

Only legal business importers who filed protests against Section 301 tariffs are eligible for the immediate $166B pool. This is a court-ordered settlement, not a stimulus check.

🛑

Individual Checks are Pending

The American Worker Rebate Act (which would pay individuals) is still in the "In Committee" phase in the US Senate. Any site claiming to offer instant individual payouts is likely a scam.
